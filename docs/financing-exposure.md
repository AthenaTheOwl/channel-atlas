# Follow the cash path before adding the edges

Method update: October 18, 2026. Proposed experiments below are not reported as executed results.

A financing graph records relationships at different levels of the same capital chain. In a synthetic example, a lender commits 100 to an SPV and the SPV commits the same 100 to a tenant. The two edges sum to 200 of gross contractual links; they do not establish 200 of distinct deployed capital. Likewise an undrawn commitment is different from a funded loan, and a lease payment schedule is different from principal.

The current graph and inbound-exposure view are useful relationship screens. Interpret their totals as gross edge amounts within the fixture, retaining each edge currency. Proposed extensions should add funded-versus-committed status, instrument identity, economic asset identity, maturity, collateral and guarantee links before claiming net exposure or losses.

A bounded next fixture is one lender funding two vehicles leased to the same tenant. Reconcile the gross edge total, unique funded principal and tenant concentration separately. Then add a guarantee without treating it as another cash disbursement. Accept the extension only if a reviewer can reproduce each aggregation from named edge ids. A source filing supports the relationship it actually describes; it does not settle accounting consolidation by itself.
